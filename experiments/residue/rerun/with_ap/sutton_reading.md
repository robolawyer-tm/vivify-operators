I am choosing a government or quasi-governmental body over a corporation. A corporate compliance filing or board-commissioned review would fit most of the coordinates. "Sovereign" and the society-wide overlay push toward a public authority whose account becomes the official version for everyone.

The gap between the inflated account and people's lived experience is fuelling the conflict, not settling it. The likely course is that suppression holds formally for a while, then fails. Leaks, media attention, legal action, or a later inquiry could each break it open. When that happens, this very document becomes evidence of a cover-up rather than of resolution. The archive that was meant to close the case keeps it open.
